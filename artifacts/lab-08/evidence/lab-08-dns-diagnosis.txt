### 3. The classic incomplete egress policy
apiVersion: cilium.io/v2
kind: CiliumNetworkPolicy
metadata:
  name: client-egress
spec:
  endpointSelector:
    matchLabels:
      run: client
  egress:
    # "I allowed the client to reach backend." Looks complete. It isn't.
    - toEndpoints:
        - matchLabels:
            app: backend
      toPorts:
        - ports:
            - port: "80"
              protocol: TCP

NOTE: the gap between retry bursts is ~2.5s. Two of those is the 5-second DNS stall.

### 6. The fix — the egress rule everyone forgets
apiVersion: cilium.io/v2
kind: CiliumNetworkPolicy
metadata:
  name: client-egress
spec:
  endpointSelector:
    matchLabels:
      run: client
  egress:
    # the rule everyone writes
    - toEndpoints:
        - matchLabels:
            app: backend
      toPorts:
        - ports:
            - port: "80"
              protocol: TCP
    # the rule everyone forgets
    - toEndpoints:
        - matchLabels:
            io.kubernetes.pod.namespace: kube-system
            k8s-app: kube-dns
      toPorts:
        - ports:
            - port: "53"
              protocol: UDP
          rules:
            dns:
              - matchPattern: "*"
