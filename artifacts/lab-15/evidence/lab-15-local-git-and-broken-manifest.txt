### 5. The part that matters: production never moved
$ kubectl -n shop get deploy storefront
NAME         READY   UP-TO-DATE   AVAILABLE   AGE
storefront   4/4     4            4           5m10s

$ kubectl -n flux-system get kustomization shop-config -o jsonpath='...Revision'
lastApplied   = main@sha1:3aacd02d043fb30c41b72cd115174af83acda1c3
lastAttempted = main@sha1:2f0c08ae100d760e54472bb99e8fb9499d024df1

-> Flux dry-runs before applying. The bad commit was refused, the last good
   revision stayed live, and the two revision fields tell you exactly that.
